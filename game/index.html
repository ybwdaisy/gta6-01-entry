SERENA BAY HEAT

A courier. A harbor boss. A city that never sleeps.

Top-down open-world crime in the sun-soaked coastal city of Serena Bay. Drive, steal, run fares and work six missions for Marta Vidal — while the Serena Bay PD keeps count of everything you do.

How to play

  • WASD / arrows — walk  ·  Shift — sprint
  • Mouse — aim  ·  Click — shoot  ·  R — reload
  • E — steal a car / talk / take a fare / repaint  ·  F — leave the car
  • Esc / P — pause  ·  M — mute
  • Follow the M marker for Marta's missions. Lose the cops at Talleres Pipo ($150 repaint) or by staying out of sight.
  • Progress autosaves when you finish a mission.
